## Header Artifacts

    - Sender email address: Where did the email come from?
    - Sender IP address: What is the ources IP, and what does a reverse lookup reveal?
    - Email subject line: Does it contain urgency or a call to action?
    - Recipient email address: Who is the intended recipient (To/CC/BCC)?
    - Reply-To email address: Where are responses being directed?
    - Date and time: When was the email sent?
    - Security checks. (SPF, DKIM) [Phishing Prevention](./2026-08-02-Phishing_Analysis_Tools.md)

## Body Analysis

    - URLs and hyperlinks: Identitfy all links and expand shortended URLs to reveal their true destination.
    - Attachment name(s): What files are included, and do their names or extensions appear suspicious?
    - Attachment hash: Generate a hash value for threat intelligence lookups.


## Email Header Analysis
[Messageheader](https://toolbox.googleapps.com/apps/messageheader/analyzeheader) part of the Google Admin Toolbox, helps analyze email headers. Paste in the full header and it will extract important information.
[Message Header Analyzer](https://mha.azurewebsites.net/) does similar analysis.


## IP and URL Reputation Analysis
[IPinfo](https://ipinfo.io/) can quickly view details such as georaphic location and associated organization.
[URLScan.io](https://urlscan.io/) safely investigae websites wihout visiting them directy. Submit a URL and the servce simulates a real user browsing session and records all activity generated by the page including screenshots. 
Talos [IP & Domain Reputation Center](https://talosintelligence.com/reputation_center/) threat intelligence tool from Cisco that enables analysts to assess he reputation of IP addresses, domain, and networks.


## Email Body Analysis
Link analysis, right click and check it out (hovering can work too), [URL extraction tool](https://www.convertcsv.com/url-extractor.htm), or [CyberChef](https://gchq.github.io/CyberChef/#recipe=Extract_URLs(false,false,false))

File hashes of attachments. sha256sum on linux, Get-FileHash in Windows PowerShell. Hash can be uploaded to Talos [IP & Domain Reputation Center](https://talosintelligence.com/reputation_center/) or [VirusTotal](https://www.virustotal.com/gui/)

## Malware Sandboxes
Safely run suspicious files. Sandboxes can generate reports for URLs contacted, registry changes, processes started, files created or deleted.
[ANY.RUN](https://app.any.run/)
[Hybrid Analysis](https://hybrid-analysis.com/)
[JOESandbox](https://www.joesandbox.com/)
[PhishTool](https://www.phishtool.com/)
